# Prompt técnico — Seedance 2.0 | Hero em loop

Use a imagem de referência como primeiro e último quadro visual. Preserve integralmente a identidade, proporções, tipografia, símbolos, cores, materiais e acabamento da arte original.

## Prompt em inglês

Create a premium cinematic website hero background video using the provided reference image as the exact visual foundation. Preserve the original logo, typography, proportions, colors, composition, shapes, materials and brand identity with no redesign and no invented elements.

Animate the scene with elegant, subtle and realistic motion suitable for a high-end technology and promotions platform: controlled depth, soft parallax, restrained light sweeps, refined reflections, gentle particles and precise micro-movements. Keep the main logo and important visual information stable, sharp and readable at all times. Use a locked or nearly locked camera, smooth motion, realistic lighting, clean edges, natural motion blur and premium commercial finishing.

The video must work as a full-screen website hero background. Keep the central safe area visually clean so interface text, buttons and navigation can remain readable over the video. Avoid aggressive movement, rapid cuts, zoom jumps, flicker, warping, morphing, duplicated objects, unstable text, deformed logos or artificial-looking details.

Create a mathematically seamless infinite loop: the final frame must match the first frame in composition, camera position, lighting, object positions, motion direction and exposure. All animated elements must complete their movement cycle naturally before the final frame. No visible transition, jump, fade to black or pause at the loop point.

Technical output: 1280x720, 16:9, 720p, 24 fps, 8 seconds, H.264-compatible visual quality, high detail, low compression artifacts, no audio, no subtitles, no captions, no watermark, no interface elements and no black bars.

## Negative prompt

Do not alter the logo or typography. No text distortion, no spelling changes, no extra symbols, no new products, no camera shake, no fast camera movement, no abrupt zoom, no scene cut, no flicker, no pulsing exposure, no heavy particles, no smoke covering the logo, no warped geometry, no duplicated elements, no low-resolution textures, no oversharpening, no artificial plastic look, no audio, no subtitles, no watermark and no visible loop transition.
